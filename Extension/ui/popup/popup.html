Normal Photo, Autopilot and Queue run local macOS Vision OCR before GPT to identify real label evidence and add compact, untrusted hints to the primary request. After the primary GPT pass, a missing direct label fact may be recovered locally only when the field is allowlisted, OCR evidence is strong, and the value exactly matches the current eBay options. Visual/ambiguous facts stay GPT-owned, and OCR never overwrites a conflicting GPT answer. Conflicts or rejected strict evidence keep one bounded GPT repair; weak or unsupported missing fields stay blank instead of causing speculative paid retries.

AI Response Length & Budget

Budget status not calculated yet.

Normal Fill has a hard OpenAI ceiling of 2 upstream requests: exactly one Primary plus at most one targeted Repair. Local OCR/CV runs first. A strongly readable local label stays LOW-detail in Primary because the Local Evidence Engine can verify/recover its direct facts for free; weak or conflicting label evidence is promoted to HIGH-detail, and any Repair re-reads only the exact unresolved evidence at HIGH detail. Missing strict fields with no direct evidence are not retried. Paid Primary photo caps remain Cheap 4 / Balanced 6 / Quality 8. The normal per-item cost target is $0.07 or less, but accuracy is fail-safe: unresolved facts are left for review rather than buying a third GPT call. Review Mode is separate and may additionally use Claude. Daily and monthly API budgets remain hard limits.
